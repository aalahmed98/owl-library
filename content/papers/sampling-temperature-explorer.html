Language models pick the next token by sampling from a softmax over logits. A single scalar — the temperature — decides whether that distribution is a near-deterministic spike or a near-uniform spread. Drag the slider and watch the same five logits produce very different distributions.

At T → 0 the largest logit takes everything (greedy decoding); at T = 1 you get the raw softmax; past T ≈ 2 the choice is close to a coin flip across all candidates.

Why entropy rises monotonically with T

The softmax ratio between two tokens is exp((z_i - z_j)/T). As T grows every pairwise odds ratio decays toward 1, so the distribution approaches uniform — the maximum-entropy distribution over the support. Differentiating the entropy with respect to T shows it is proportional to the variance of the logits under the current distribution, which is non-negative.
